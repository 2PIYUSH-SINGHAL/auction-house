Auction sign-in

The auction mini-event is a closed-room bidding game. Each registered team is issued a private Auction ID and a Passcode — use them to enter the hall.

Find it

Your Auction ID and Passcode were emailed to the team's teacher-in-charge after registration.

Lost it?

Email auction@welham.edu.in from your school address.

§ House Rules
  1. One sign-in per team. The session locks this device once entered.
  2. Bids are final. No retractions, no re-rolls.
  3. Doors close at 20:55 IST sharp. Late entries forfeit their float.
  4. Disputes go to the auctioneer. Their word is law.
Need help?

Ping a volunteer in the hack.welham help desk channel on Zoom, or email auction@welham.edu.in before doors open at 20:55 IST. After that, flag the auctioneer directly in the session.
